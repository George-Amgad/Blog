About This Blog

BU Tech Blog is dedicated to raising awareness about cybersecurity threats, starting with phishing attacks. Our goal is to help you recognize scams, understand how attackers operate, and learn straightforward steps to protect your data.

Why Phishing?

Phishing remains one of the most common and effective online scams. Even tech-savvy users can fall for a well-crafted email or website. By breaking down techniques and sharing prevention tips, we hope you’ll feel more confident online.
