What Is a Phishing Attack?

A phishing attack is a type of social engineering where attackers impersonate trustworthy entities to steal sensitive data like usernames, passwords, or credit card details.

Common Techniques

  • Email spoofing: Fake sender addresses.
  • Malicious links: URLs leading to fake login pages.
  • Clone phishing: Replicating legitimate emails.

How to Spot Phishing

  1. Check the sender’s email domain carefully.
  2. Hover over links before clicking (look at the real URL).
  3. Look for urgent or threatening language.

Prevention Tips

Always verify requests for personal information by contacting the company directly through official channels. Keep your software and browser up to date.
